# Legal

Conservative guidelines, not legal advice.

- **Knots themselves** (how a bowline is tied) are traditional and free for anyone to show.
- **Drawings and text** are this app's own. Never trace or copy another knot site's or
  book's animations, diagrams or wording (for example Animated Knots or Grog's): draw each
  knot fresh from how it is tied, and write the steps in our own words.
- **Code**: only our own, plus dependencies with permissive licenses (`npm run licenses`
  checks them; `scripts/check-licenses.mjs` lists the allowed ones). The app ships no
  third-party code at run time other than the service worker Workbox generates (MIT).
- **Names**: knot names are generic. No product names or logos.
